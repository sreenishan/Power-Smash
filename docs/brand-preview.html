Text needs 4.5:1, large text (24 px, or 19 px bold) and controls need 3:1. The key rules: volt is never text on a light background (about 1.2:1); the brief's border colour is for dividers only, never the only edge of a field or button; secondary text on dark uses the derived mist colour, not slate; red on dark is never text (use a red fill with white text).
