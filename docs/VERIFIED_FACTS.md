# VERIFIED_FACTS - third-party facts behind the Power Smash Phase 1 plan

**2026-10-22 hosting note (D-097):** hosting moved from Cloudflare Workers to Vercel on the owner's decision. Section I (VF-V1 to VF-V9) holds the Vercel facts; rows below that describe Workers, `wrangler`, `workers.dev`, Cron Triggers or the 20,000-file cap describe the retired plan and are kept as history. Turnstile, Web Analytics and Email Routing rows still apply.

**As of 2026-10-20, with section 1b and rows VF-A27 to VF-A32 and VF-C26 added on 2026-10-21 after Milestone M0, section G (VF-G1 to VF-G22) added on 2026-10-21 after Milestone M1, and section H (VF-H1 to VF-H9) added on 2026-10-22 after Milestones M2 and M3 (each carries its own as-of date).** Every other row comes from the six research reports, the six independent skeptic re-checks (verify) and the four audits in the verification team's scratchpad. Where a skeptic corrected or refuted a researcher, the **corrected** value is used here. Nothing in this file is legal, tax or financial advice; items marked CA or LAWYER need a chartered accountant or an Indian lawyer.

How to read the columns

- **Basis**: `official` = read on the vendor or government page; `official (summariser)` = official page but read through a summarising fetch tool; `3rd-party summary` = law-firm, blog or news summary only (the primary document was not readable); `registry/API` = a live registry or feed lookup; `inference` = reasoning on top of documents.
- **Confidence**: the confidence stated after verification.
- **Status vs spec**: `confirmed` (the spec is right), `changed` (the spec or the first research answer was wrong or outdated and the plan changes), `unverified` (no authoritative answer found; the plan carries a test or an owner/lawyer/CA question).
- Source URLs are the ones the researcher or skeptic cited. A re-check is only as strong as its source: rows with `3rd-party summary` should be re-read in the primary text (Gazette, vendor page) before anyone relies on them for money or legal decisions.
