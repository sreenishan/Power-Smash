# SPEC_AUDIT - what is wrong, missing or unsafe in `power-smash-website-spec.md` (v1.0, 2026-10-20)

This file merges and de-duplicates four independent audits (feasibility `F-`, privacy-security `SEC-`, revenue `REV-`, parser-data `PD-`) with the findings of six research reports and six skeptic re-checks (YouTube `YT-`/`MF-`, Supabase `SB-`, stack-hosting `STK-`/`MISS-`, payments `PAY-`/`MISSED-`, India legal `LEG-`/`MISS-`, services `EM-`/`CF-`/`DOM-`/`AFF-`/`WA-`/`LIB-`). Each row gets one id, the spec section, the problem, and **what the plan does about it** (a decision in `DECISIONS.md`, a milestone in `BUILD_PLAN_PHASE1.md`, or "OWNER DECISION"). The last column keeps the original ids so nothing is lost.

Reading guide

- Severity follows the audits: **critical** = the plan as written fails, harms people or cannot be tested; **major** = needs a design decision or a new requirement before build; **minor** = fix while building.
- `D-0xx` = decision in `DECISIONS.md`. `M0`-`M4` = milestones in `BUILD_PLAN_PHASE1.md`. `VF-xx` = row in `VERIFIED_FACTS.md`.
- "OWNER DECISION" means Claude Code must not decide alone; the question is in the plan's decision list.
- Nothing here is legal advice. LAWYER and CA mark where a professional must confirm.

Counts: 16 critical, 52 major, 27 minor, plus 30 parser corrections (section 5) and the missing requirements (section 6). Milestone M0 added two major and two minor findings, kept apart in section 8 (SA-M53, SA-M54, SA-N28, SA-N29) so the original counts stay traceable. Milestone M1 added the 58 findings of three independent reviews (12 major, 46 minor; 15 more minor findings at the retest) and 14 new audit lines, kept apart in section 9 (finding ids `S-`, `B-`, `C-`; audit lines SA-M55 to SA-M68).
