# Coverage agreement - DRAFT for a lawyer to review (OT-15, D-019)

**Status: draft template. Not legal advice. Not reviewed by a lawyer. Not for use until a lawyer has reviewed it and the owner has filled the blanks.** Prepared 2026-10-21 by the Legal-drafts worker (Milestone M1).

How to read it: text in **[FILL: ...]** is a blank that the owner (and the CA for money and tax) must supply. No fee, price, percentage, date or tax rate is proposed anywhere in this draft. **[LAWYER: ...]** marks a point on which the lawyer must decide or confirm. The numbered clauses are a suggestion for structure; the lawyer may restructure freely.

Why it exists (D-019): the BAI Competition Regulations (an undated edition; status unknown) put commercial, television, internet, webcasting and film rights at BAI-sanctioned tournaments with BAI, and BAI currently grants them to the promoting Affiliated Unit; that unit must obtain any legally required player consent, and "the user of photographs or other such images of player(s) assumes sole legal liability" (cl. 3.10 to 3.15, VF-E16). Under Copyright Act 1957 s.17(b), a film made for valuable consideration at the instance of another person has that person as first owner unless the contract says otherwise (VF-E15). So every paid coverage booking needs a written permission and an ownership clause, from the first booking. The website's `/book` form already asks whether permission has been agreed (yes, no, unsure) and the tournament record has a `filming_permission` field; this agreement is the document behind it.

One agreement per event. Where the organiser is a promoting association or Affiliated Unit, the association signs, or gives a letter of authority (Schedule C).
