# Video-submission licence - DRAFT for a lawyer to review (OT-15, D-019)

**Status: draft. Not legal advice. Not reviewed by a lawyer. The "send your video" form (`/submit-video`) is built in Milestone M3; this text is drafted now so the lawyer reviews all licence wording together.** Prepared 2026-10-21 by the Legal-drafts worker.

How to read it: **[FILL: ...]** is a blank for the owner (and CA for any money term). **[LAWYER: ...]** is a point the lawyer must decide. No payment, fee, royalty or percentage is proposed here.

Where the short wording lives: the three tick-boxes the visitor sees are the consent texts `video-rights`, `video-licence` and `video-removal`, plus `adult-confirmation`, in `src/content/consent.ts`, each with an id and a version. This document is the longer licence they point to. Every submission stores: the version of each text, a timestamp, a salted IP hash, and the one-time-code or emailed acceptance (below), so the exact wording accepted can be shown later.

Why a tick-box alone is not enough (D-019, VF-E15): Copyright Act 1957 s.19 requires an assignment to be in writing signed by the assignor, with a default term of five years and India only if none is stated. Whether a ticked web box is a "signed writing" for a copyright licence has not been tested. So the form adds a one-time-code or emailed acceptance in addition to the box, and the owner downloads a copy of the submitted file, with a hash, at approval. **[LAWYER: is this enough? Is a signed form needed for some submissions?]**

## 1. Who is giving the licence

The person submitting ("you"), whose name, role (player, parent or guardian, coach, organiser, other), phone and email are recorded on the form. You must be **18 or older** (box `adult-confirmation`). If you are the parent or guardian of a player who appears in the video, say so in the form.

The licence is given to **Power Smash**, **[FILL: legal name of the business]** ("we").

## 2. What you confirm (before anything is accepted)

2.1 **You recorded the video yourself, or you have the right to share it** (box `video-rights`). It is **not broadcast or third-party footage**, and it **does not contain copyrighted music**.

2.2 **Who owns the footage** (a question on the form): I filmed it myself as a spectator / I was hired or asked by the organiser / the organiser owns it / other **[FILL: wording]**. If you were hired or asked by the organiser, or the organiser owns the footage, you give us the organiser's name and their written permission **[LAWYER: what evidence is enough, for example a WhatsApp or email message]**.

2.3 **Players under 18.** You tell us whether players under 18 can be recognised in the video (yes, no, unsure). If yes or unsure, we review it more strictly and ask for the confirmation of a parent or guardian before we publish. **[LAWYER: confirm the parent-confirmation method; the project's default is a one-time code from the parent's own phone, D-015.]**

2.4 You will not send injury, misconduct, abuse or dispute footage. We may reject any video for any reason, and sending a video does not mean we will publish it.

## 3. The licence you give us (box `video-licence`)

You give us a **non-exclusive** licence, **anywhere in the world**, **for as long as the video stays on the Power Smash YouTube channel**, to:

(a) publish the video on our YouTube channel and on our website;
(b) earn money from it there (YouTube advertising, memberships and similar, and sponsorship shown beside it);
(c) make reasonable edits for publishing: trimming, a title and labels, our own watermark, and our standard "Match details by Power Smash" information **[LAWYER: confirm the scope of edits and whether to allow a highlights compilation]**;
(d) keep a copy of the file for our records and to deal with removal requests or disputes **[LAWYER: confirm how long, and whether to say "for as long as the law requires"]**.

3.1 **You keep your copyright.** Nothing in this licence transfers ownership to us. You may use the video yourself and licence it to others. **[LAWYER: confirm "non-exclusive" is the right default, and whether to offer an exclusive option for money, which the owner has not decided: [FILL: none / terms]].**

3.2 **Credit.** We may credit you by name on the page ("Video: your name") unless you ask us not to (the licence box says so). We do not have to credit you in the video itself.

3.3 **Payment.** **[FILL: none; or any fee, revenue share or reward the owner decides, with the CA. Nothing is promised in this draft.]**

3.4 **Moral rights, warranties, indemnity.** **[LAWYER: moral rights waiver or consent for edits; your warranty that the video does not infringe anyone's rights; whether to ask for an indemnity from submitters and how to word it for ordinary parents and players.]**

## 4. Removing a video (box `video-removal`)

4.1 We will **remove the video** (set it to private or unlisted, or delete it, and take it off our website) if a **player, a parent or guardian, or a rights holder** makes a **valid request**, following our Content Removal and Takedown Policy (acknowledge within 24 hours, decide within 7 days; hide first for a player under 18; act within 48 hours for a child; act on a lawful court or government order within 3 hours; these are our targets). **[LAWYER: confirm.]**

4.2 **You may also ask us to remove your video** at any time. We will do so **[FILL/LAWYER: within what time, and any limit if the video is already part of a paid product or a sponsor package]**.

4.3 Removal ends our licence for new use. We cannot recall copies that already exist elsewhere (search-engine caches, messaging apps, other people's channels).

- The four boxes are separate and never pre-ticked (`adult-confirmation`, `video-rights`, `video-licence`, `video-removal`).
- After you send the form, we ask you to confirm by a **one-time code sent to the phone number you gave, or by a link sent to your email**. **[LAWYER: confirm this is enough as a written acceptance.]**
- We store: your submission, the version of each text you accepted, the time, a salted hash of your IP address, and the confirmation. We keep the submission record until it is resolved and for **[FILL/LAWYER: period; the privacy policy gives the project's default of the request or submission record until resolved plus 12 months, then redaction]**.

## Open points for the lawyer (also in the briefing pack, question B7)

1. Non-exclusive worldwide licence for the life of the video on the channel: right default, and is the stated term compatible with Copyright Act s.19 (term and territory must be stated)?
2. Acceptance method: tick-box plus one-time code or emailed link, versus a signed form.
3. Minors in submitted videos and the parent-confirmation route.
4. Who may submit footage from an event whose rights belong to BAI or an Affiliated Unit (BAI Competition Regulations cl. 3.10 to 3.15): does a spectator's own clip need the organiser's permission to be monetised?
5. Warranty, indemnity and edit scope.
